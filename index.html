Case study

Content Refresh Scoring

The problem

A content team has more pages than they can manually review each week, and no reliable way to know which ones actually need attention first.

What I did

Ran the starter ML pipeline and saw a hand-written rule only get 24% of its top picks right, while a trained model got about 74%. That gap is what led me to the Refresh / Content Opportunity Scoring lane — framed as a ranking problem, not classification, since a reviewer only has capacity for a batch, so precision at the top matters more than being right about every page. I used current decline trend as a proxy target, since I don't have real "this needed a refresh" labels yet.

When I checked how many pages were both stale and actually visible in search, it was tiny — 17 out of 16,726 visible pages, about 0.1%. Staleness turned out to be a much weaker signal than I expected.

What came of it

A working baseline pipeline and a clearly framed ML task — not a deployed tool yet. I know what I'm building and why, backed by real numbers instead of assumptions.
